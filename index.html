About

I am interested in Chinese Buddhist grottoes and in finding ways to share their history and art with a wider audience. This page is a small portfolio sample.

Selected work

The Complete Works of Chinese Grottoes is a public-facing illustrated book published by Huazhong University of Science and Technology Press. It introduces the cultural and artistic significance of China's Buddhist cave temples, from the Wei and Jin dynasties to the Yuan and Ming periods.

As one of the primary writers and editors, I contributed roughly one-third of the text (around 200 pages), focusing on sites such as Kizil, Piyang, Anyue, and Maijishan. My work included synthesizing excavation data, verifying iconographic details, and adapting archaeological records into accessible Chinese narratives for the public.
